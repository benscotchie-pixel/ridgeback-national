# Ridgeback National

A fictional golf course in Lake Wales, Florida, built from scratch with Claude Code
by Ben Scotchie for *AI and the Future of Work* (Fall 2026).

- **Website:** https://benscotchie-pixel.github.io/ridgeback-national/
- **Watch Me Play (3D):** https://benscotchie-pixel.github.io/ridgeback-national/watch.html
  (open in Chrome, give it a few seconds to load, pick a hole and press Watch this hole)

The website looks like a real golf club's site, and every photo on it is a render of
the 3D course. Watch Me Play is a TV-style broadcast of me playing the course at my
handicap, with my swing taken from AI pose tracking of my range videos.

## What's where

- `website/` – the club website
- `watch.html`, `src/` – the 3D course, golf simulation, golfer and TV broadcast
- `assets/` – textures and sky
- `tools/` – tools used to make the website photos and track my swing

Built with three.js, Poly Haven textures (CC0), MediaPipe pose tracking and Claude Code.
