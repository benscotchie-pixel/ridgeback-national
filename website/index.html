Welcome

Ridgeback National is an 18-hole public course laid across the Lake Wales Ridge, the ancient sand spine that runs down the middle of Florida. Here the land rolls in a way the rest of the state doesn't, with live oaks and longleaf pine framing every hole and spring-fed ponds catching the evening light.

Every round starts on a first tee 40 feet above the 18th green and ends on a porch overlooking it. In between are four par 5s you can reach, three short par 4s that reward thinking over power, and a set of par 3s played over water and down off the ridge. Four sets of tees make it a fair walk for every golfer, from a first-timer to a scratch player.

The Course

Par 72 · 6,947 yards · Four sets of tees

The routing follows the ridge's natural rise and fall, so no two holes play in the same direction for long or from the same elevation.

Fairways are wide where the land asks you to swing freely and tight where it asks you to think. Greens are firm, fast and tilted, rolling around 11 on the Stimpmeter most of the year. Choose your tees below to see each hole's yardage.

On the course

Course policies

  • Collared shirts and tailored shorts or slacks. No denim on the course.
  • Soft spikes only.
  • Pace of play is 4 hours and 15 minutes for 18 holes. Player assistants are on the course to help.
  • Carts stay on paths at all par 3s and within 30 yards of every green.
  • Walking is welcome at any time.
Booking

Changes & weather

  • Cancel or change up to 24 hours before your tee time at no charge.
  • Late cancellations and no-shows are charged the full green fee.
  • Rain checks are issued for play stopped by weather, prorated by holes completed.
  • Lightning detection is active: when the horn sounds, leave the course right away.

Membership information

Our members come from every corner of Central Florida and every walk of life. Some play five days a week. Some come for Friday dinner on The Porch and a Sunday nine with the kids. What they share is a love of the game and of the people they play it with.

Ridgeback National is a privately owned club that welcomes public play, with a limited membership for golfers and families who want to call it home. Members enjoy privileges according to their membership type, including the golf course, practice facility, dining rooms and porch, social and family events, and the club's full calendar of leagues and tournaments.

Our story

Old ground, new course

A million years ago the Lake Wales Ridge was a chain of islands in a shallow sea. Today it is some of the highest, sandiest and best-draining ground in Florida, which is exactly what you want under a golf course.

The property spent most of the last century as a cattle ranch and citrus grove. When the grove came out, the owners walked the land with a simple idea: build a course that anyone can play, that feels like it has always been here. Fairways were laid where the sand already wanted them, the ponds were dug where water already collected, and nearly every live oak on the property was kept.

The course opened in 2026 as a public course with no initiation fee and no gate. Its four sets of tees run from 4,940 to 6,947 yards, so a family can play together on the same afternoon.

The land

Ridge sand

Deep, sugar-white sand drains fast after a summer storm, so the course rarely closes for rain and fairways stay firm enough to bounce a ball onto the green.

The design

Width and angles

Wide fairways with the trouble on one side reward the golfer who picks the right line, not just the longest one. Most greens are open in front for anyone who wants to run the ball on.

Stewardship

Kept wild

More than 300 acres of scrub and pine flatwoods stay untouched around the course, home to gopher tortoises, scrub jays and the occasional bobcat on the 14th.
